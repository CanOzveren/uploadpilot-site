Content publishing & scheduling

Publish on schedule, with authorization.

UploadPilot is a private content publishing and automation tool for authorized operators of social media channels. It helps organize publishing workflows and schedule video uploads through supported platform APIs.

How it worksPrivacy policy

How UploadPilot works

Every action is performed on accounts that the operator has explicitly authorized.

01

Authorize

The account owner grants access through the platform's own consent screen. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform.

02

Organize

Videos and their metadata are prepared and arranged into publishing workflows.

03

Schedule

Uploads are scheduled for the times the operator chooses.

04

Monitor

Publishing status is tracked so the operator can see what was published and what needs attention.

Platform integrations

UploadPilot connects to social media platforms through their supported, official APIs. Which platforms are active may change over time.

UploadPilot is an independently operated tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, YouTube, Meta, Instagram, or TikTok. Platform names are used only to describe compatibility and belong to their respective owners.

YouTube API Services

UploadPilot uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos on channels the operator owns, to read the status of those uploads, and to read the operator's own channel analytics. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy. Access is granted through Google's consent screen and can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. UploadPilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
